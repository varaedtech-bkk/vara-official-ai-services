# TutExperts (tutexperts.com)

## What TutExperts is
TutExperts is VARA EdTech's live tutoring marketplace and classroom, at https://tutexperts.com. VARA EdTech operates it and owns the TutExperts software and brand. TutExperts is a product name, not a separate legal company. Students, tutors, schools, and tutor agencies all use the same platform.

## Who it is for
Four sides share one platform. Learners book 1:1, group, trial, live, or instant help. Independent tutors teach and get paid after verified completion. Tutor Agencies onboard tutors and may earn about a 5% supply share on those tutors' lessons. Institutions and schools hire existing marketplace tutors for class coverage and never earn that supply commission.

## What students and tutors can do
Students find tutors by subject, level and language, book a trial or paid lesson, join a live classroom with video, whiteboard, screen share and chat, message the tutor, share files, and track history. Group seats are typically 2–50 students at a per-seat price. Tutors apply with subjects, bio and credentials; the TutExperts team reviews them before they go live. Payouts after completed lessons go through Stripe Connect, PayPal, Wise or Payoneer depending on region. The live site currently mentions 5,000-plus learners and 50-plus subjects.

## Institution coverage (schools)
Schools apply as an Institution, pass review, pay a one-time onboarding fee, then hire substitutes per lesson. The card is authorised at booking and captured after the lesson. There is no prepaid wallet and no supply commission for Institutions. Coverage roster has a soft limit around 50 learners. Roster Pro unlocks CSV import, classes and attendance. Campus LMS adds assignments and grading. Campus LMS uses TutExperts branding, not a custom university brand. Parent portals and a full student-information-system replacement are not available today.

## How to join
Students sign up on tutexperts.com. Tutors use Become a Tutor. Schools and agencies use organisation signup: https://tutexperts.com/organization-signup. Support typically replies in 1–2 business days for login, payment, refund and onboarding issues.

## Payments, policy and trust
Checkout uses Stripe. Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link when enabled; regional methods include PromptPay, TrueMoney, GrabPay and Alipay where offered. Institution coverage authorises at booking and captures after the lesson. Cancellation on paid marketplace lessons: more than 24 hours full refund, 12–24 hours half, under 12 hours none. The site offers a full refund if someone does not love their first lesson. Personal data is processed under Thai PDPA.

## Locales
The product UI ships 19 locales including English, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Portuguese, Spanish, Arabic, Urdu, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Russian, Italian, Lao, German, Malay, Japanese, Korean, Bengali and French.

## Honest limits
Staff notes: do not quote a firm TutExperts subscription price from memory. Do not promise white-label campus branding, parent portals, full SIS, or a custom TutExperts build on the first conversation. If they want a school pilot or a similar marketplace for their brand, take details and let the team confirm. SIS sync is not live.
