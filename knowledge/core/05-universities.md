# What VARA offers universities

This is the core offer when talking to a university, a faculty, a rector's office or a student body.

## The opening frame
"We build AI that helps students learn, saves your team hours every day, and makes your university feel modern and effortless."

## Why now — the four pressures on a university
1. **Students want fast answers.** They live on apps that reply in seconds. Slow replies are felt, and some applicants walk away.
2. **Staff repeat the same work.** The same questions, forms and follow-ups every day — work AI can handle quietly in the background.
3. **Insights arrive too late.** By the time a report is ready, the moment has passed. AI can show what matters right now.
4. **Messages get lost.** Email, calls, chat and social all live in different places, so things slip. One AI hub keeps it together.

# The pilot path — how a partnership actually starts

## Three steps
1. **We talk.** A short, friendly chat to find the one thing that would help the campus most. VARA listens and maps it out.
2. **We build a pilot.** A real working version for that one job, in the university's own branding, put in real hands quickly.
3. **We grow.** Once it works, roll it wider and add the next idea — building the AI campus step by step.

## Typical timeline
- **Phase 1 (months 1–2):** stakeholder alignment and partnership formalisation → first workshop or pilot build → impact assessment and feedback.
- **Phase 2 (months 3–4):** second workshop or expanded pilot based on demand → mentorship programme launch if applicable.
- **Phase 3 (month 5+):** regular workshops, student AI community support, faculty development, curriculum advisory.

For a software pilot specifically: 2–4 weeks of discovery and build → go live → review at 30 / 60 / 90 days → then decide on scaling.

## What the university provides
Venue with projector and internet · student promotion and recruitment · a designated coordination contact · help gathering post-session feedback.

## What VARA delivers
Complete workshop content and materials · qualified facilitators · hands-on exercises and demonstrations · participant resources and follow-up materials · ongoing communication and support.

## VARA's promise
"You always see value before you commit further. We stay focused on real results: happier students, and real time saved for your team."

## Proposed MoU scope (when a university wants to formalise)
- Named faculties or departments in the pilot cohort
- A designated university champion plus a communication SLA
- Data roles (controller vs processor) and a privacy addendum
- Success metrics — for example response time to student enquiries, enquiries handled, staff hours saved, student satisfaction
- An optional upgrade path after pilot review
- Timeline: pilot (4–6 weeks) → evaluation → MoU renewal or expansion

## Honest limits to state when asked
- Internal maturity scores are not third-party certifications.
- Controller/processor roles and student/minor data need legal review on both sides.
- A dedicated University MoU template and an enterprise DPA / subprocessors pack are prepared per engagement rather than off the shelf.
- White-label campus branding for the TutExperts Campus LMS, parent portals, full SIS replacement and net-30 invoicing are not current product claims.
