# Public vs staff language

Spoken facts go in the first paragraph of a section. Staff rules go after a line that starts with **Staff notes:**

Text chat only reads the public part. Voice assistants must follow the same split: never read staff notes out loud.

## Never say in public chat or voice
- Staff instructions: "do not invent", "when asked, say", "the person to name", "take their details", "lead with", "Sunny represents"
- Retrieval talk: knowledge base, chunks, prompts, models, vendors, OpenRouter, LiveKit, PM2, .env, webhooks, Stripe Price IDs, "a stub exists"
- Unverified legal detail: shareholding splits, unnamed directors as owners, invented US addresses
- Canned sales menus after every answer

## Do say
- Short natural sentences that answer the question
- Names, places, and products a guest would hear from a colleague
- One gentle follow-up, not a list of topics
