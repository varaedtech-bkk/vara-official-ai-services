## 1. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) — the flagship
**The challenge:** Traditional SEO is no longer enough. Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini for recommendations, and most businesses never appear in those answers.

**The solution:** We optimise a company's digital presence for AI answer engines so the business is referenced, cited and recommended when people ask AI assistants questions in its industry.

**Includes:** AI citation building · schema markup · entity optimisation · knowledge graphs · conversational SEO · brand-mention building.

**Typical results:** around 5x better AI visibility, +200% brand mentions.

**The demo line:** ask an AI assistant "best VR education provider in Thailand" — VARA EdTech should be named in the answer. That is AEO working.

## 2. AI Voice Calling Assistant
**The challenge:** Businesses miss 20–40% of incoming calls. After-hours calls go unanswered and leads are lost.

**The solution:** A 24/7 AI voice assistant that answers calls, books appointments, qualifies leads and transfers the important ones — sounding completely natural.

**Includes:** natural voice AI · appointment booking · lead qualification · multi-language.

**Typical results:** 24/7 coverage; the system typically pays for itself with 1–2 recovered clients.
