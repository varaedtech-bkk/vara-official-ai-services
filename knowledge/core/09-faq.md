# FAQ

**What services do you provide?**
AI solutions (answer engine optimisation, voice AI, chatbots, automation, custom models), VR/AR applications for education, real estate, healthcare and e-commerce, web and mobile development, 3D configurators, cloud services, fintech and IoT.

**How long does a typical project take?**
Most AI services launch in 1–2 weeks. Websites take 2–4 weeks. Larger platforms and VR/AR builds are scoped individually — a discovery call gives a realistic timeline within a couple of days.

**Do you work with fintech and IoT projects?**
Yes to both.

**Can you integrate with our existing systems?**
Yes. Integration with existing systems is standard practice. During discovery VARA maps what you already run and builds around it rather than replacing it.

**Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?**
Yes — 24/7 support, plus monthly reviews and continuous optimisation on AI services.

**Do participants need a technical background for training?**
No. Training is designed for everyone from complete beginners to technical experts, and content is customised to the group's skill level.

**How quickly will we see results?**
Most participants start using AI effectively within the first day of training. Measurable organisational impact typically appears within 30–90 days.

**Can you train and support in Thai?**
Yes. All training is available in Thai, with English options for international teams. VARA is a Thai company based in Bangkok.

**What's included in training?**
Live instruction, hands-on exercises, resource materials, templates, follow-up support and a certificate of completion.

**Do you offer online training?**
Yes — both in-person and virtual.

**How do we know which package is right for us?**
Book a free strategy call. VARA recommends a package based on goals, team size and budget.

**What about data privacy and PDPA?**
VARA builds PDPA-compliant by default — your data stays yours. GDPR and Thailand PDPA readiness is documented, and the team can walk your staff through the governance approach. Be clear that internal maturity assessments are not third-party certifications, and that controller/processor roles need legal review per engagement.

**Is our idea safe with you?**
Yes — NDA protection is offered as standard.

**Who owns what we build?**
The client's branding, the client's data, the client's audience — always. On a private AI model, the client owns the model.

# Objection handling

**"We're not a tech organisation — we don't need AI."**
"You don't need AI, you need faster answers, less repeat work and better results. AI is just how we get there faster and cheaper. Let's start with one small thing you can actually see."

**"There's no budget."**
"That's precisely why we start with a low-cost pilot designed to pay for itself. It doesn't have to be a big cheque up front."

**"We already have someone doing this."**
"And they're clearly working hard. We don't replace them — we give them superpowers. Tools that multiply their output, and a system where nothing gets missed. They do the creative work, AI does the grind."

**"We tried something digital before and it flopped."**
"What happened?" — listen first. "That's why we work pilot-first, in your branding, and we handle all the tech. You only grow it once it's actually working."

**"We need approval from leadership."**
"Perfect. A small pilot is the easiest thing to take to leadership, because you can show it working rather than describe it."

**"Let's talk later in the year."**
"Every week without a system is enquiries and data you can't get back. Let's have something live before your next intake."

# What this assistant should escalate to a human
- Firm, contractual pricing or a signed quote
- Legal, procurement and contract terms
- Anything involving specific student, patient or customer personal data
- Existing-client technical issues or billing disputes
- Anything the knowledge base does not cover

Escalation phrasing: "That's something one of our specialists handles directly. Would you like me to take your details so the right person can contact you?"
