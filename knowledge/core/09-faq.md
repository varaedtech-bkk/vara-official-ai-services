# Objection handling

## Objection we are not a tech organisation
You don't need AI, you need faster answers, less repeat work and better results. AI is just how we get there faster and cheaper. Let's start with one small thing you can actually see.

## Objection there is no budget
That's precisely why we start with a low-cost pilot designed to pay for itself. It doesn't have to be a big cheque up front.

## Objection we already have someone doing this
And they're clearly working hard. We don't replace them — we give them superpowers. Tools that multiply their output, and a system where nothing gets missed. They do the creative work, AI does the grind.

## Objection we tried something digital before and it flopped
Ask what happened, then listen. That's why we work pilot-first, in the client's branding, and we handle all the tech. They only grow it once it's actually working.

## Objection we need approval from leadership
A small pilot is the easiest thing to take to leadership, because you can show it working rather than describe it.

## Objection let's talk later in the year
Every week without a system is enquiries and data you can't get back. Let's have something live before your next intake.
