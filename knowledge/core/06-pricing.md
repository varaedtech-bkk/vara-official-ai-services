# Pricing and commercial model

## The rule for talking about price
Prices depend on scope. VARA shares an honest range first, then confirms a real figure after a free consultation.

Staff notes: never quote a firm price as final, never invent a number that is not in this document. Framing: "It depends on scope, so let me give you the honest range — and then we scope a real number for you for free."

## Software and AI build projects
Custom builds (AI assistants, AEO programmes, chatbots, platforms, VR/AR applications) are scoped individually. What VARA can say confidently:

- **Most AI services launch within 1–2 weeks.** Websites take 2–4 weeks.
- **Typical cost reduction from AI automation: 40–60%.**
- **Start small.** VARA's method is one small pilot, in the client's branding, proving value before scaling. This keeps the first cheque small.
- **Flexible structures are available** — a modest fixed pilot fee, or in some cases a revenue-share arrangement so risk is shared rather than paid up front.
- **Ongoing support** is offered as monthly review and optimisation.

## The free evaluation
Every engagement can start with a free AI evaluation:
1. **Free AI Evaluation** — VARA analyses the business or institution to identify which AI solutions make the most sense
2. **Custom Proposal** — a specific proposal with transparent pricing and a realistic timeline
3. **Quick Implementation** — most services launch within 1–2 weeks with minimal disruption
4. **Ongoing Support** — monthly reviews, continuous optimisation and dedicated support

## Handling "there's no budget"
"That's exactly why we start with a low-cost pilot designed to pay for itself. We can even structure it so it isn't a big cheque up front. Pick the one thing that would help you most this term — we build it, you watch it work, and we grow from there."

## Handling "how do we know you'll deliver?"
"Fair question. We're a registered Thai company with live products and signed MoUs. And we prove it your way: a small pilot you see working before you commit further."
